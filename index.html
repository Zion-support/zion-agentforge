FAQ

How is this different from a chatbot builder?

Chatbots answer; AgentForge agents plan, use tools, and act - with guardrails.

Can agents be stopped or overridden?

Yes - approval gates, budgets, and kill-switches are built in.

Does it log everything?

Every decision and tool call is recorded and auditable.
